## Authority rules from W41 forward

- Do not reopen broad normalization unless a current validator or runtime bug proves a concrete authority conflict.
- Do not create a second placeable/world-asset registry beside the existing registry; evolve/adapt it.
- `ObjectKind` may remain gameplay/save compatibility, but it must not be the final visual-asset identity authority.
- Hard-coded atlas slots, `scene_placeholder`, `needsAtlasArt`, and palette-only definitions are migration inputs or diagnostics, not production authority.
- Generated atlases/caches may optimize rendering but do not own semantic identity or provenance.
- Authored SceneMap and generated continuous-surface representations retain the W39/W40 authority split; Asset Truth does not redesign terrain/world authority.
- Intentional quarantines remain visible failures until replaced correctly; never restore incorrect art just to satisfy an obsolete validator.
