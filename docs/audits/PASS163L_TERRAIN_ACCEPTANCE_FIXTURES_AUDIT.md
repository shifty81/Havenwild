# Pass 163L Terrain Acceptance Fixtures Audit

## Fixtures

1. Coastline — curved water, wet sand, sand, and grass boundary.
2. River — variable-width channel with turns and mud banks.
3. Farm soil — grass, dirt, tilled, and watered soil boundary.
4. Mountain — cliff, rock, and traversable mountain path.
5. Snow/ice — layered deep water, shallow water, shore, stone, and land.
6. Wrapped seam — mirrored edge bands with horizontal wrapping enabled.

## Safety

These maps are diagnostic-only and are not inserted into the production scene
registry. They cannot silently alter the mainland, saves, or normal scene flow.
