## Commercial policy

Preferred: CC0, CC-BY, OGA-BY.
Conditional: CC-BY-SA with preserved attribution and share-alike handling for modified art.
Rejected by default: NonCommercial, GPL-only, unknown, conflicting, or incomplete provenance.
