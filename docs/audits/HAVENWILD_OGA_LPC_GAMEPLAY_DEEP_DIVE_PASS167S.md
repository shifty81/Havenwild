## Normalized gameplay ownership

Art does not define simulation. Stable data definitions own crop timing, animal production, fish habitat, tree yields, building interactions and forage respawn. LPC sheets provide reviewed visual bindings and animations.

## High-value gameplay coverage

- farming: 50 crop growth cycles, orchard growth, barns, coops, apiaries and processing stations;
- ecology: seasonal deciduous trees, snowy conifers, flowers, fungi, bushes, stumps and logs;
- livestock: chicken, cow, pig, sheep and llama walk/eat foundations;
- fishing: four-directional visible fish for local schools and hooked-fish feedback;
- world generation: biome/season-tagged vegetation and farmstead visual sets;
- commerce/crafting: harvest items and farm-processing machines linked to recipes and storage networks.

## Intake rule

Downloaded archives go to WORKSPACE intake storage first. They are promoted into `content/assets/oga_lpc/promoted` only after bundled credits, dimensions, duplicate hashes, anchors and game bindings pass review.
