# Havenwild V7 + ElizaWy Cliff and F3 Terrain Audit — Pass 167Z61

## Scope

This pass audits the V7 and ElizaWy terrain lanes as separate style packs, identifies every current cliff-authoring path, and reshapes the F3 terrain workflow so horizontal ground materials cannot be confused with structural cliff faces.

## Confirmed root cause of the light-grey F3 cliff tile

Two independent legacy paths produced the symptom:

1. `BuildTool::Floor(TileKind::Cliff)` exposed `Cliff` as a normal ground brush.
2. The F3 height generator converted every exterior cell between the mountain threshold and its upper band into `TileKind::Cliff`.

The renderer then used V7 `Mudstone_Brown` as a compatibility fill for that tile. The result was a flat light-grey/brown ground square, not a vertical cliff.

Pass 167Z61 removes the direct brush and removes `TileKind::Cliff` from exterior height classification. High terrain now remains `MountainRock` horizontal ground. Structural faces continue to derive from neighboring elevation differences through `ElevationCliffV2`.

## V7 terrain lane audit

V7 contains 34 semantic material families:

- tan, brown, dark dirt;
- white, gray, dark, and black rock;
- brown and black pits/holes;
- mud, soil, gravel, rooted dirt, and cracked earth;
- normal, light, dark, and dead grass;
- sand and snow variants;
- shallow/deep/colored water, ice, melting ice, and lava;
- white/tan stone and gray/brown mudstone.

These give V7 strong coverage for temperate islands, drylands, dark forests, rocky highlands, snow/ice regions, volcanic or hazardous regions, and caves. They are horizontal surfaces, transitions, pits, and hazards. They are **not** a complete 2.5D vertical cliff-face set. V7 therefore provides plateau tops and cliff-adjacent ground, while a separately audited style-coherent face provider is required for vertical elevation faces.

Authority: `content/worldgen/v7_terrain_role_catalog_v0_1.json`.

## ElizaWy terrain lane audit

The pinned ElizaWy repository audit covers 64,365 files at commit `f07f7f5892e67c932c68f70bb04472f2c64e46bc`. The terrain lane contains five seasonal ground/water sheets, five seasonal cliff sheets, tilled soil, ice shallows, waterfall art, and a dedicated rock/cliff-detail sheet.

The five 512×448 seasonal cliff sheets were checksum-verified and inspected directly. All five use the same 16×14-cell alpha geometry. The sheet includes:

- rounded and square plateau assemblies;
- bare and grass-topped faces;
- straight, curved, diagonal, inner, and outer forms;
- cave openings and wide tunnel mouths;
- vine-covered faces;
- ladder and bridge/walkway face treatments;
- water-facing banks and cliff sections.

The art is authored as connected multi-cell assemblies. It must not be treated as 224 unrelated 32×32 paint tiles. The new per-cell catalog records occupancy and review regions for all 224 cells while keeping runtime promotion gated until exact connected-region recipes, anchors, stacking, and structural collision bindings are certified.

Authorities:

- `content/worldgen/elizawy_terrain_role_catalog_v0_1.json`
- `content/worldgen/elizawy_cliff_sheet_role_catalog_v0_1.json`
- `content/worldgen/elizawy_tile_lane_isolation_contract_v0_1.json`

## Structural cliff authority

A cliff is now defined as an elevation relationship, not a ground material:

- plateau top: horizontal style-pack ground;
- face: derived from a drop of at least two height units;
- blocked approach: derived from the opposite side of that edge;
- ramps: derived from a one-unit walkable height change;
- cave hosts and waterfall hosts: derived from structural topology and depth checks.

`TileKind::Cliff` remains only as a saved-map compatibility value until old data is fully migrated. It is not available in the normal F3 ground palette and is not generated by the exterior height painter.

## F3 terrain workspace changes

The F3 tabs are now organized as:

`World | Ground | Props | Zones | Elevation | Topology | Rules | Sources`

- **Ground** paints reviewed horizontal semantic materials only.
- **Elevation** raises, lowers, and smooths the height field and explains the distinction between the highland rockline and the structural face-step delta.
- **Topology** inspects neutral shared shape masks and style-specific bindings.
- **Sources** owns provenance, sheet grids, audit state, and promotion gates.

The misleading `Cliff-` and `Cliff+` controls are renamed `Highland-` and `Highland+`. They change the absolute rock-ground threshold; they do not control the relative cliff-face delta.

## Save migration

Client terrain generation version advances from 7 to 8. On the next load of an older exterior save:

- legacy flat Cliff cells near sand or water normalize to Grass;
- remaining legacy exterior Cliff cells normalize to MountainRock;
- structural faces remain derivable from saved heights.

## Remaining implementation lane

This pass fixes terrain authority, data audits, F3 organization, new-world classification, and old-save normalization. It does **not** falsely activate unfinished cliff rendering. The next dedicated cliff pass must map the ElizaWy connected regions to `StructuralCellV2`, render stacked faces/corners/ramps, bind collision to structural edges, and certify cave entrances, waterfalls, ladders, and bridge attachments.
