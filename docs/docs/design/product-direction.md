# Product Direction

## Working Title

Havenwild Prototype

## Pitch

A cozy but deep tavern-builder RPG with first-class construction tools, live content editing, greenhouse/farming systems, brewing/cooking pipelines, and mod-like content packs built into the game from day one.

## First Playable Loop

1. Move around the tavern and farm.
2. Open the tavern.
3. Customers enter, sit, and generate reputation/coin.
4. Enter construction mode.
5. Place tables/chairs/kegs/beds.
6. Convert floor/grass to tilled dirt.
7. Paint greenhouse zones that override ordinary crop restrictions.

## Systems To Build

- Construction validation: room bounds, door/access paths, comfort, lighting.
- Greenhouse zones: define area, require build resources, enable year-round crop beds.
- Farming: till, plant, water, grow, harvest, process.
- Production: recipes, ingredient tags, fermentation, aging, keg capacity.
- Tavern service: customer needs, dirt/mess, seating, serving queue, checkout.
- Staff: hire, schedule, assign stations, wages, traits.
- Content packs: JSON definitions for items, recipes, furniture, crops, NPCs, and dialogue.
- In-game editor: map, sprite/pixel tools, object inspector, script/event hooks.

## Tone

Warm, practical, busy, and workshop-like. Cozy but not sleepy: the fantasy is running and expanding a living tavern with tools that feel powerful.
