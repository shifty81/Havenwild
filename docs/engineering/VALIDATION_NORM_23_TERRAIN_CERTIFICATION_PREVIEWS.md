# VALIDATION-NORM-23 — Terrain Certification Preview Triplet

The terrain certification acceptance images are a single generated evidence family:

- `semanticTopologyPreview`
- `lpcMappedPreview`
- `comparisonPreview`

All three are optional in current-source integrity and may be required/regenerated by explicit
historical/release certification. Their underlying terrain topology, material, mapping, recipe and
source contracts remain mandatory.

The `terrain_render` / `bridge_render` requirements were inventoried but intentionally left unchanged
until their surrounding contracts are classified rather than guessed.
